PodLever Pricing Model — v2 (Revised)
Core Principle
PodLever sells orchestration and workflow, not raw AI.

Users pay a platform fee for the system (reliability, versioning, asset management, approvals, FSM, dashboard, etc.).
AI processing is handled in one of two ways:
BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) → User pays providers directly.
Managed AI Credits → User buys compute from PodLever at a marked-up rate.


This eliminates the cannibalization problem in v1.

text# PodLever Pricing Model v2
Status: Draft for review
Date: 2026-07-18

## Philosophy
PodLever sells orchestration, reliability, and time savings — not raw AI.
Users can either bring their own AI keys (BYOK) or buy Managed AI credits from us.

## Platform Tiers
- Free: $0 / 3 episodes (BYOK only)
- Creator: $39 / 8 episodes (BYOK)
- Professional: $99 / 20 episodes (BYOK + Managed AI credits)
- Studio: $249 / 50 episodes (BYOK + Managed AI + multi-show/team features)

## Managed AI Credits
- $6.00 per episode
- Bulk packs available at a discount

## Unit Economics (Planning Baseline)
- Estimated fully-loaded COGS per 60-min episode (Managed AI): $2.00
- Target gross margin on Managed AI: 65–75%
- BYOK users have very high platform margins (~95%)

## Strategic Notes
- Platform fee is charged regardless of AI mode
- Managed AI is a convenience/upsell, not the core product
- Free tier is limited but sufficient to experience value
- Pricing emphasizes professional workflow over "AI episodes"

## Open Items for Future Review
- Annual billing discount (likely 15–20%)
- Team seat pricing
