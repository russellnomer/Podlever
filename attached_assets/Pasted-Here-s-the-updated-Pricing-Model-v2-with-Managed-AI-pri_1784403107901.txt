## Philosophy
PodLever sells orchestration, reliability, and time savings — not raw AI.
Users can bring their own AI keys (BYOK) or purchase Managed AI credits.

## Platform Tiers
- Free: $0 / 3 episodes (BYOK only)
- Creator: $39 / 8 episodes (BYOK)
- Professional: $99 / 20 episodes (BYOK + Managed AI credits)
- Studio: $249 / 50 episodes (BYOK + Managed AI + multi-show/team features)

## Managed AI Credits
- $7.00 per episode
- Bulk packs available at a discount

## Unit Economics (Planning Baseline)
- Estimated fully-loaded COGS per 60-min episode (Managed AI): $2.00
- Target gross margin on Managed AI: 65–75%
- BYOK users generate very high platform margins (~95%)

## Key Decisions
- Platform fee is fully decoupled from AI compute
- Managed AI is a convenience/upsell, not the core offering
- $7.00 price point chosen for better margin protection vs $6.00
- Free tier set at 3 episodes to improve chance of reaching “aha” moment
